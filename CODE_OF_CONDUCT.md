# Code of Conduct

This project follows the principles of the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/). We aim to provide a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

Project participants are expected to:

- Be considerate, constructive, and respectful in all project spaces.
- Critique ideas and code rather than people.
- Accept feedback graciously and focus on what benefits the community.
- Avoid harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, and unwanted personal attention.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing another person's private information.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct harms the community. To report an incident, contact the maintainers privately through the repository's GitHub contact options. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical.

The complete Covenant text and enforcement guidance are available at the linked version 2.1 document.
